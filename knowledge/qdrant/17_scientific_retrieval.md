# Scientific Retrieval

---

# What is Scientific Retrieval?

Scientific retrieval means:

```text
retrieving scientific knowledge semantically
```

instead of relying only on:

```text
manual exploration
keyword matching
static databases
```

Modern AI systems increasingly attempt to:

* explore experiments semantically
* connect scientific observations
* retrieve related phenomena
* assist scientific reasoning

Scientific retrieval is one of the most promising applications of RAG systems.

---

# Why Scientific Retrieval Matters

Scientific datasets are increasingly:

* large
* multimodal
* complex
* interconnected
* difficult to explore manually

Traditional search often becomes insufficient.

Semantic retrieval helps scientists navigate knowledge spaces.

---

# Traditional Scientific Search
